Privacy Policy — ACE

Last updated: 15 October 2026. Contact: princeclyde428@gmail.com

ACE is a personal, single-user desktop assistant. It is used only by the account owner who authorizes it; it is not offered to the public and does not process data for anyone else.

What data ACE accesses

  • Google account (YouTube, Gmail, Calendar) — only the permissions granted on Google's consent screen: uploading and reading the owner's own YouTube videos and comments; reading, sending and replying to the owner's own Gmail; reading the owner's own calendar.
  • WhatsApp — read and send messages through the owner's own linked device session.
  • Local files and settings — the assistant stores its notes, tasks, reminders and connection tokens on the owner's own computer.

How data is used

Only to carry out the owner's own requests — for example posting a video the owner asked to post, summarising the owner's unread email, or replying to the owner's comments and messages. Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Where data is stored

On the owner's own computer. Sign-in tokens are stored locally and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or from WhatsApp's Linked devices screen. Revoking access immediately stops ACE from using that account.

Data retention and deletion

The owner can delete the assistant's local data at any time, which permanently removes stored messages, notes and tokens.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address.
