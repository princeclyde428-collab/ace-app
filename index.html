ACE

ACE is a personal desktop assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It helps with everyday tasks and, with the owner's explicit permission, connects to the owner's own accounts to read and act on their behalf — for example posting videos to their YouTube channel, reading and replying to their Gmail, checking their calendar, and reading their WhatsApp messages on their linked phone.

ACE is a single-user application: the only person who uses it is the account owner who authorizes it. It does not serve other users, and access is granted and revoked by the owner through the standard Google sign-in consent screen.

See the Privacy Policy for how data is handled.
